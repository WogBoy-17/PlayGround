BUILD YOUR SQUAD
Recruit Zoids with scrap. Click a shop card to buy. Units land in your reserve bay; select one, then an open tile to deploy it. Select a deployed Zoid and another tile to reposition. Click your unit again to inspect or sell it.

WIN THE WILD TERRITORY
Your team fights automatically. Melee units move into range; ranged Zoids attack from a distance. Win encounters to earn scrap and command XP. Duplicate Zoids combine into a stronger ★★ unit.

FORMATIONS MATTER
Matching Zoid frames unlock squad bonuses. You can deploy up to your command level. Lose all 3 core integrity and the campaign ends.
